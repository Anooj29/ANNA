🚀 End-to-End SIH 2026 Demonstration Workflow

Follow this live 5-step sequence during demonstration to showcase the unified database and real-time automation:

1
Admit Patient
Go to Receptionist, click an available bed, take a photo with face verification, and click Register.
2
Doctor Queues Checkup
In Clinician Portal, select the newly registered patient and click "Assign Visit" (Normal or Urgent).
3
ANNA Bedside Checkup
Either physical robot claims task or click "Simulate Checkup" to perform the AI vitals & Q&A routine.
4
Doctor Analytics
Doctor dashboard updates instantly via WebSocket showing vital trends, emotion distribution, and clinical report.
5
Patient Review
Log into Patient Portal with the patient's code and PIN to view the friendly health report and medications.
